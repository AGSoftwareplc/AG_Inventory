Getting started

AG Inventory follows how goods move through the shop. You buy from suppliers, receive the goods into stock, sell them at the POS, and then check money and reports.

  1. Starter logins for each role:
    • Administrator: admin / admin123
    • Sales Attendants: cashier / cashier123, cashier2 / sales222, cashier3 / sales333
    • Inventory Manager: storekeeper / store123
    Each person should change their password straight away with Password at the top right. The administrator can rename these users or add more in ⚙ Admin → Users.
  2. Set up the shop: click ⚙ Admin at the top, then Settings & backup, and enter the shop name, address, phone and payment methods. These appear on receipts.
  3. Add your staff: in ⚙ Admin → Users, give each person a role. A Sales Attendant sells. An Inventory Manager buys, receives and counts stock.
  4. Add products: use Products → New product. For goods already on the shelf, enter the Opening stock and Purchase cost.
  5. Start working: sell with Sell (POS), and buy with Purchases & receiving.

The menu on the left is in the order of daily work. It shows only what your role is allowed to do. On a phone, tap ☰ to open it. Each area has its own colour: Selling green, Buying orange, Stock blue, Money & reports purple and Administration grey. Click a group title to fold it open or shut. Click Narrow menu « at the top to show icons only (point at an icon to see its name), and » to widen it again. The menu remembers your choice.

Help: click ? Help or press F1 on any page to read about that page.

Selling at the POS

  1. Add products: scan the barcode, or type part of the name or code and press Enter. Use the ↑ ↓ keys to choose another match, or click it. Scanning the same product again adds 1 to its quantity.
  2. Change a quantity: type it in the Qty box. Decimals are allowed, for example 2.5 kg. To remove a line, click ×.
  3. Discount (only if your role allows it): enter an amount or a percentage (%).
  4. Amount paid: enter the cash received and the change is shown. Leave it empty if the customer pays the exact total.
  5. Click Complete sale, then Print receipt.

Credit sale (customer pays later): choose a registered customer and enter the part they paid now. You take the rest later on the sale page.

A red "only X in stock" label means there isn't enough stock. The sale can't be completed until you lower the quantity.

Clicking Complete sale twice by mistake doesn't record the sale twice.

Sales history, returns and cancellations

  • Sales history lists sales by date. Filter by Unpaid / credit to see customers who owe money. Click a receipt number to open it.
  • Receive a payment (credit sale): open the sale, enter the amount and click Save payment.
  • Customer return: open the sale, choose the item and quantity, and enter a reason. The items go back into stock. The refund is the item's price after its share of any discount. The screen tells you how much cash to give back.
  • Cancel a sale (managers only): open the sale, enter a reason and click Cancel sale. All items go back into stock. A sale that already has returns can't be cancelled.
  • Reprint a receipt: open the sale and click Receipt.

Sales are never deleted. Every correction is recorded with the user and the reason.

Customers

Register customers who buy on credit or buy often. Each customer page shows their purchase history and how much they owe. You can use walk-in sales without registering anyone.

Buying stock: order, receive, pay

  1. Purchases → New purchase: choose the supplier and add product lines with the quantity and unit cost. Add the supplier's discount if there is one.
    • Save as draft: you can still change it, and stock isn't affected.
    • Save and confirm order: the order is final.
  2. When the goods arrive: open the purchase, check the Receive now quantities and click Receive into stock. If only part arrived, enter what actually came. The rest can be received later, and the status shows Partially Received.
  3. Pay the supplier: on the same page, use Record payment to supplier. Partial payments are fine, and the balance owed is shown.
  4. Return to supplier: for damaged or wrong goods, choose the line and quantity and enter a reason. Stock and the amount owed both go down.

Cancel: a purchase can be cancelled only before anything is received. After that, use a return.

Cost: the product cost is updated automatically as a weighted average. A purchase discount is spread over the lines.

Suppliers

Add each supplier with a contact person and phone number. The supplier page shows all purchases and the total amount the shop still owes them. You can deactivate a supplier you no longer use.

Stock levels and movements

  • Stock levels shows every active product with its quantity, minimum level and value at cost. Rows highlighted in yellow are at or below their minimum, which means it's time to reorder. The yellow bar at the top of every page counts them.
  • Stock movements is the full history: every receiving, sale, return, adjustment and count, with the date, user, reference and balance after it.

Stock changes only through these transactions, never by typing a number directly.

Products and categories

  • New product: enter a unique code, the name, category, unit (pcs, kg, litre…), selling price and minimum stock level. A barcode is optional.
  • Several products at once: click + Several products. Type one product per row. A new empty row appears as you type in the last one, and × removes a row. Click Save all products. If a row has a mistake, such as a missing price or a code that is already used, nothing is saved yet. That row turns red with the reason, and everything you typed stays in place.
  • Opening stock: for goods already in the shop, enter the quantity and purchase cost once, when you create the product.
  • Change a price: edit the product. Old sales keep their old prices, and price changes are logged.
  • Stop selling a product: untick Active. A product that has been used can't be deleted.
  • Categories: group products, for example Drinks or Food. Use them in searches and reports.

Adjusting stock

Use this for stock that leaves the shop without a sale:

  • Damaged, Expired or Lost: enter how many to remove.
  • Correction: a positive number adds and a negative number (for example -2) removes.

A reason is always required. Every adjustment is saved with your name and appears in the Damaged / expired / lost report.

Stocktake: counting the shelves

  1. Click Start new count, for all products or one category.
  2. Click Print count sheet if you want to count on paper.
  3. Type the counted quantities. Leave a product empty to skip it. Click Save counts as often as you like.
  4. Check the Difference column, then click Post stocktake. Stock is set to the counted quantities, and each difference is recorded as a stocktake correction.

Sales made while you are counting are taken into account when you post.

Branches and transfers

Each branch has its own stock, sales, purchases, expenses and stocktakes. The branch you work in is shown in the top bar; if you work in more than one branch, pick it there. Products, prices, suppliers and customers are shared by all branches. Document numbers start with the branch code, for example MAIN-S-000042.

  1. To move stock, open Transfers in the branch that sends it and click Send stock to another branch.
  2. Choose the branch and the products, then click Send now. The stock leaves the sending branch at its average cost and shows as In transit.
  3. At the other branch, open the transfer, check the quantities that arrived and click Receive. Anything that did not arrive is recorded as lost in transit.
  4. If the goods never leave, the sending branch can Cancel the transfer and the stock comes back.

Expenses

Record running costs such as rent, electricity, salary or transport: choose the category, amount, date and payment method. You can add new categories on the same page.

Mistake? Click Reverse on the wrong entry and give a reason. A negative entry cancels it, and you then enter the correct expense. Expenses are never deleted.

Reports

Choose a report, set the dates and filters, then click Show.

  • Inventory: current stock and value, low stock, movements, damaged/expired/lost, stocktake differences.
  • Sales: by day, week or month; by product, category or user; returns; cancelled sales; customer balances.
  • Purchases: history, by supplier, by product, outstanding supplier payments.
  • Financial: profit summary, expenses, payment collection by method.

Excel downloads the report as an .xlsx file. For a PDF, click Print / PDF and choose Save as PDF as the printer.

Profit: gross profit = sales after discounts and returns − the cost of the goods sold. Net operating profit = gross profit − expenses.

Dashboard

The dashboard shows today's figures: sales, purchases, expenses, gross and net profit, stock value, low-stock products and best sellers. Change the dates to see another period. Staff without report rights see only counts, not money figures.

  • Sales per day: one bar per day. With only one day chosen, it shows the last 14 days. For more than two months, it shows one bar per month. Point at a bar to see the amount.
  • Where the money went: net sales, the cost of the goods sold, expenses and the profit that is left.
  • Best-selling products: the longest bar sold the most.
  • How customers paid and Stock value by category: the share of each part. Small parts are grouped as Other.
  • Low stock: how full each product is compared with its minimum. Red means none left.

Administration (administrators only)

Open it with the ⚙ Admin button at the top of every page, or with Users, settings & backup at the bottom of the side menu. The tabs at the top of the page switch between the sections below.

  • Users: create one login per person. To stop someone logging in, untick Active. To reset a password, type a new one.
  • Role permissions: tick what Sales Attendants and Inventory Managers may do, for example give discounts, cancel sales or see profit.
  • Settings: shop details for receipts, currency, payment methods, receipt footer.
  • Backup: a copy of the data is made automatically every day. Click Make and download a backup now and keep copies on a USB stick.
  • Restore: choose a backup file and click Restore backup. Everyone then logs in again.
  • Audit log: shows who did what, such as logins, price changes, cancellations, returns and adjustments.

Questions and problems

Other computers or phones can't open the app.
Use the address shown in the black start window, for example http://192.168.1.10:5000, on the same Wi-Fi. Allow Python through the Windows firewall for private networks. The start window must stay open.
"Not enough stock" when selling.
The goods were not received into the system. Receive the purchase, or correct the stock with Adjust stock.
"This form was already submitted".
The page was sent twice, for example by a double click or a refresh. The first submission was saved, so nothing is missing.
"Form expired".
Reload the page and try again.
I see "Not allowed".
Your role doesn't have that permission. Ask the administrator.
Forgot a password.
An administrator can set a new one in Users.
