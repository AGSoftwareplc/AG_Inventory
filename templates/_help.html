Getting started

Mizan follows how goods move through the shop. You buy from suppliers, receive the goods into stock, sell them at the POS, and then check money and reports.

  1. Starter logins for each role:
    • Administrator: admin / admin123
    • Sales Attendants: cashier / cashier123, cashier2 / sales222, cashier3 / sales333
    • Inventory Manager: storekeeper / store123
    A red warning shows on the dashboard until these passwords are changed. Each person should change their password straight away: click Password at the top right, type the current password, then the new one twice (at least 6 characters). The administrator can rename these users or add more in ⚙ Admin → Users.
  2. Set up the business: click ⚙ Admin at the top, then Business profile & backup. Enter the business name, address, phone, email, TIN, logo, currency, payment methods and units of measure. The logo and details appear in the top bar, on receipts and on printed reports.
  3. Branches (if you have more than one shop): add them in ⚙ Admin → Branches. See Branches and transfers.
  4. Add your staff: in ⚙ Admin → Users, give each person a role and tick the branches they work in. A Sales Attendant sells. An Inventory Manager buys, receives, counts and transfers stock.
  5. Add products: use Products → New product, or + Several products to type many at once. For goods already on the shelf, enter the Opening stock and Purchase cost.
  6. Start working: sell with Sell (POS), and buy with Purchases & receiving.

The menu on the left is in the order of daily work. It shows only what your role is allowed to do. On a phone, tap ☰ to open it. Each area has its own colour: Selling green, Buying orange, Stock blue, Money & reports purple and Administration grey. Click a group title to fold it open or shut. Click « next to the blue Sell (POS) button to show icons only (point at an icon to see its name), and » to widen it again. The menu remembers your choice.

The top bar shows the business logo and name (set them in ⚙ Admin → Business profile & backup; until a logo is uploaded, the initials of the name are shown), the branch you are working in (📍), ⚙ Admin for administrators, ? Help, Password and Log out.

Help: click ? Help or press F1 on any page to read about that page. Open full manual / print shows the whole manual on one page.

Selling at the POS

  1. Add products: scan the barcode, or type part of the name or code and press Enter. Use the ↑ ↓ keys to choose another match, or click it. Scanning the same product again adds 1 to its quantity.
  2. Change a quantity: type it in the Qty box. Decimals are allowed, for example 2.5 kg. To remove a line, click ×.
  3. Discount (only if your role allows it): enter an amount or a percentage (%).
  4. Amount paid: enter the cash received and the change is shown. Leave it empty if the customer pays the exact total.
  5. TT No. / payment reference: for Telebirr, bank or mobile banking payments, type the transaction number. You can search sales by it later.
  6. Click Complete sale, then Print receipt.

Change the price (only roles with Change the selling price during a sale): type the new price in the cart. The product's default price stays unchanged and is kept beside the price sold, with the difference and your name. See the reports Sold above / below the default price and Price variation by product.

Serial numbers (phones, laptops and other products with serial numbers): scan the serial number in the search box, or add the product and then scan or pick each serial number under its name. The quantity is the number of serial numbers. A serial number that is already sold or not in stock is refused.

Credit sale (customer pays later): choose a registered customer and enter the part they paid now, or 0 if they pay nothing now. The sale is then Partially paid or Unpaid. You take the rest later on the sale page. Only roles with the Sell on credit permission can do this.

The goods leave stock as soon as the sale is completed, whether it is paid or not. The stock is taken from the batch that expires first, then from the oldest batch (FIFO).

The POS sells from the stock of the branch shown in the top bar, and the receipt number starts with that branch's code.

A red "only X in stock" label means there isn't enough stock. The sale can't be completed until you lower the quantity.

Clicking Complete sale twice by mistake doesn't record the sale twice.

Sales history, returns and cancellations

  • Sales history lists sales by date with their payment status: Paid, Partially paid or Unpaid. Use the Status box to show only one of them, or Owing for every sale with money still due. You can search by receipt number, customer or TT No. Click a receipt number to open it.
  • Receive a payment (credit sale): open the sale, enter the amount (part payments are fine), the method, the TT No. if there is one and the date, then click Save payment. The balance owed goes down. A payment never creates a new sale or changes stock.
  • Payment history: the sale page lists every payment with its date, method, TT No. and who received it.
  • Batches used: users who can see profit also see which stock batches the sale used and their cost.
  • Customer return: open the sale, choose the item and quantity, and enter a reason. The items go back into stock, into the same batch they came from. The refund is the item's price after its share of any discount. The screen tells you how much cash to give back.
  • Cancel a sale (managers only): open the sale, enter a reason and click Cancel sale. All items go back into stock. A sale that already has returns can't be cancelled.
  • Reprint a receipt: open the sale and click Receipt.
  • Manual sale (roles with Record manual sales): Selling → Manual sale records a sale made outside the app, for example on paper. Enter the real date, who actually sold, the products, prices and payment. Say whether the stock must be deducted now (FIFO) or was already taken out, so it is never deducted twice. A manual sale gets an M receipt number, a purple Manual label, and keeps both the seller and the person who recorded it. Use Recorded in Sales history to show only POS or only manual sales.
  • Serial numbers: to return a serial-numbered item, type its serial number in the return form.

Sales are never deleted. Every correction is recorded with the user and the reason.

Customers

Register customers who buy on credit or buy often. Each customer page shows their purchase history with the payment status of each sale, every payment they made (with TT No.) and how much they owe. You can use walk-in sales without registering anyone. The Outstanding customer balances report lists everyone who owes money.

Buying stock: order, receive, pay

  1. Purchases → New purchase: choose the supplier and add product lines with the quantity and unit cost. Add the supplier's discount if there is one. For goods with an expiry date, enter it on the line. For serial-numbered products, type or scan one serial number per item.
    • Save as draft: you can still change it, and stock isn't affected.
    • Save and confirm (adds to stock): this ends the purchase. Everything on it goes into stock at once; there is no separate receiving step. Each line becomes a stock batch with its date, quantity, cost and expiry date (see Stock batches).
  2. Pay the supplier: on the same page, use Record payment to supplier. Partial payments are fine: enter the amount, method, TT No. / reference and date. The balance owed and the payment status are shown.
  3. Return to supplier: a separate transaction after the purchase is confirmed. Open the purchase, enter the quantity to send back and a reason on each line (and the serial numbers for serial-numbered products), then click Record return. It gets its own number (PR-…) linked to the purchase. The goods leave the batch that this purchase brought in, so stock and the amount owed both go down. You can't return more than was received and is still in stock from this purchase.

Cancel: only a draft can be cancelled. Once confirmed, use a return.

Purchases confirmed with an older version, before receiving became automatic, still show Receive into stock.

Cost: each delivery keeps its own cost (FIFO). The average cost shown on a product is the value of its batches still in stock. A purchase discount is spread over the lines.

Suppliers

Add each supplier with a contact person and phone number. The supplier page shows all purchases, the payments made to them (with TT No.) and the total amount the shop still owes them. The Outstanding supplier balances report lists every supplier the shop owes. You can deactivate a supplier you no longer use.

Stock levels and movements

  • Stock levels shows every active product with its quantity, minimum level and value at cost in the branch you are working in. Rows highlighted in yellow are at or below their minimum, which means it's time to reorder. The yellow bar at the top of every page counts them for this branch.
  • Stock movements is the full history of this branch: every receiving, sale, return, adjustment, count and transfer, with the date, user, reference and balance after it.

Stock changes only through these transactions, never by typing a number directly.

Stock batches (FIFO) and expiry dates

Every time stock comes in, it becomes a batch: a delivery on a purchase, opening stock, a transfer from another branch, or extra stock found in a count. Each batch keeps where it came from, the date received, the quantity received, the quantity still in stock, its unit cost and, if you enter one, its expiry date.

  • Which batch is sold first: batches with an expiry date go first, the one that expires soonest first. Then the oldest batch received goes first (First In, First Out). The system does this by itself. If one batch is not enough, it takes the rest from the next one.
  • Cost of a sale: the cost of the batches actually used. If an old delivery was cheaper, its lower cost is used until it is sold out. Profit reports use these costs.
  • Stock batches page (Stock → Stock batches (FIFO)): all batches in stock in this branch. Choose Expiring soon to see what expires in the next days; expired batches are red.
  • A batch page shows its full history: received, sold, returned, written off, with the cost. There you can set or change the expiry date, or click Write off from this batch.
  • The product page lists its batches in the order they will be sold.
  • Returns and cancellations put the goods back into the batch they came from. Transfers take the batches with them to the other branch.
  • Reports: Stock batches (FIFO) and expiry and Cost of goods sold by batch (FIFO).

When this feature was installed, the stock already in each branch became one opening batch per product at its average cost, and a copy of the data was saved first in the backups folder.

Products and categories

  • New product: enter a unique code, the name, category, unit (pcs, kg, litre…), selling price and minimum stock level. A barcode is optional. To add your own units, such as sack, an administrator adds them to Units of measure in ⚙ Admin → Business profile.
  • Several products at once: click + Several products. Type one product per row. A new empty row appears as you type in the last one, and × removes a row. Click Save all products. If a row has a mistake, such as a missing price or a code that is already used, nothing is saved yet. That row turns red with the reason, and everything you typed stays in place.
  • Unit: choose it from the list. Quantities can have decimals, for example 2.5 kg or 0.75 litre.
  • Opening stock: for goods already in the shop, enter the quantity and purchase cost once, when you create the product. It goes into the branch you are working in.
  • Shared by all branches: the product list and selling prices are the same in every branch. Each branch has its own stock of them.
  • Change a price: edit the product. Old sales keep their old prices, and price changes are logged.
  • Stop selling a product: untick Active. A product that has been used can't be deleted.
  • Categories: group products, for example Drinks or Food. Use them in searches and reports.
  • Import from Excel: on Products (or Categories) click ⬆ Import from Excel, download the template, fill it in and upload it (Excel .xlsx or CSV). The file is checked first: every row shows OK, Duplicate (code or barcode already used) or Error with the reason, and a summary counts them. Click Import to save the correct rows. Download Rows to correct, fix them in Excel and upload that file again.
  • Product page: click a product name to see its stock and batches, every sale (date, receipt, quantity, price sold and difference from the default price, seller, TT No. and batches used), every purchase, all stock movements and its serial numbers.
  • Serial numbers: tick Track serial numbers on the product for goods where every item has its own number. Serial numbers are then entered when buying and chosen when selling; each one is unique and its page shows its whole life (purchased, sold, returned). For stock that was already in the shop, register the numbers on the product page, tab Serial numbers.

Adjusting stock

Use this for stock that changes without a sale or purchase. There are two tabs, and you always type a normal (positive) quantity:

  • + Add stock: stock you found or a count that was too low. Enter how many to add.
  • − Remove stock: choose Damaged, Expired, Lost or Correction (the count was too high) and enter how many to remove.
  • Serial-numbered products: type the serial number of each item added or removed.
  • Batch (optional): choose the batch it concerns, for example the batch that expired. When removing, leave it on Automatic to use FIFO (the batch that expires first, then the oldest).

A reason is always required. Every adjustment is saved with your name and appears in the Damaged / expired / lost report.

Stocktake: counting the shelves

  1. Click Start new count, for all products or one category.
  2. Click Print count sheet if you want to count on paper.
  3. Type the counted quantities. Leave a product empty to skip it. Click Save counts as often as you like.
  4. Check the Difference column, then click Post stocktake. Stock is set to the counted quantities, and each difference is recorded as a stocktake correction.

Sales made while you are counting are taken into account when you post.

Branches and transfers

Each branch has its own stock, sales, purchases, expenses and stocktakes. The branch you work in is shown in the top bar; if you work in more than one branch, pick it there. Products, prices, suppliers and customers are shared by all branches. Document numbers start with the branch code, for example MAIN-S-000042.

  1. To move stock, open Transfers in the branch that sends it and click Send stock to another branch.
  2. Choose the branch and the products, then click Send now. The stock leaves the sending branch from its oldest batches (FIFO), at their cost, and shows as In transit. The receiving branch gets the same batches, with the same cost and dates.
  3. At the other branch, open the transfer, check the quantities that arrived and click Receive. Anything that did not arrive is recorded as lost in transit.
  4. If the goods never leave, the sending branch can Cancel the transfer and the stock comes back.

Pharmacy module

For a business that is a pharmacy. An administrator switches it on in Admin → Business profile & settings → Pharmacy module. With it off, nothing below is shown. Everything else (purchases, POS, receipts, batches, branches, reports) works as usual.

  • Medicines: on the product form, tick This product is a medicine and fill in the generic name, strength, dosage form, brand, manufacturer, class (OTC, prescription only, narcotic, psychotropic, precursor), storage (❄ = cold chain) and the EFDA registration number and expiry. A medicine whose registration has expired cannot be bought or sold.
  • Receiving: every medicine line on a purchase needs its batch number and expiry date. Expired stock cannot be received.
  • Selling: stock is taken from the batch that expires first (FEFO). Expired, recalled, quarantined or damaged batches are never sold. OTC medicines are sold at the POS as usual. Prescription-only and controlled medicines are refused at the POS: they are sold from a prescription.
  • Prescriptions (Pharmacy → Prescriptions): 1) register it: patient, prescriber, medicines with dose, frequency and duration, and a scan (required for controlled medicines, with the EFDA form serial). 2) a pharmacist verifies it: allergy and duplicate-therapy warnings appear and need a reason to go on, or the pharmacist rejects it. 3) the pharmacist dispenses: all or part of the quantities, with payment. This makes a normal sale and receipt, and prints the dispensing labels. Controlled medicines need a second person to confirm with their own username and password. A prescription can be dispensed for 30 days (7 for controlled medicines), set in Admin settings.
  • Pharmacists: give the user the Pharmacist role (or the Verify and dispense permission) and, in Admin → Users, their licence number and expiry. Without a valid licence they cannot verify or dispense. A warning shows 30 days before it expires.
  • Patients are the people the medicine is for (age, sex, weight, which is required under 12, allergies, pregnancy, conditions), kept apart from paying customers. Their prescription history is shown to pharmacists and administrators only, and every view is logged.
  • Customer returns of medicines go into a Quarantined batch. A pharmacist inspects it and sets it back to Available, or disposes of it, on the batch page.
  • Expiry & batches: expired stock, stock expiring within 30, 60 and 90 days, blocked batches, and registrations expiring within 60 days. A yellow bar at the top warns about expired and soon-expiring batches.
  • Recalls: register the medicine and batch number. That batch is blocked in every branch, and the page lists every sale, customer and patient (with phone) that received it, to export and to record each call.
  • Disposal: from a batch, record the quantity, method, reason, two witnesses (one a pharmacist) and the certificate. The stock is written off. Disposals of controlled medicines form the destruction register.
  • Controlled register: for each narcotic or psychotropic medicine, every movement in and out with the running balance, patient, prescriber and pharmacist. It cannot be edited. Print it for the periodic report, and check the balance with a stocktake at least weekly.

Expenses

Record running costs such as rent, electricity, salary or transport: choose the category, amount, date and payment method. You can add new categories on the same page.

Mistake? Click Reverse on the wrong entry and give a reason. A negative entry cancels it, and you then enter the correct expense. Expenses are never deleted.

Reports

Choose a report, set the dates and filters, then click Show.

  • Inventory: current stock and value, low stock, stock batches and expiry dates, movements, damaged/expired/lost, stocktake differences.
  • Sales: by day, week or month; by product, category or user; returns; cancelled sales; sales by payment status (paid, partly paid, unpaid); customer payments with TT No.; outstanding customer balances; cost of goods sold by batch.
  • Purchases: history, by supplier, by product, outstanding supplier payments, outstanding supplier balances, supplier payments with TT No.
  • Financial: profit summary, expenses, payment collection by method.
  • Also: selling above / below the default price, price variation by product, POS and manual sales, manual sales with seller and recorder, returns to suppliers, serial numbers.
  • Branches (administrators, with more than one branch): sales and profit by branch, stock by branch.

Branch: reports show the branch you work in. If you work in more than one branch, choose another in the Branch box. Administrators can also choose All branches.

Daily report (Money & reports → Daily report): everything on one day (or a few days): sales (cash, bank and mobile, paid / partly / unpaid, POS / manual, by seller, price variations), purchases and returns, stock received / sold / returned / written off / adjusted, expenses, profit and outstanding balances. Filter by seller, POS or manual, and payment method. Click a receipt or purchase number to open it.

Colours: positive amounts such as profit are green with a +, negative amounts such as losses are red with a −. All amounts use a comma for thousands, for example 1,000.00.

Excel downloads the report as an .xlsx file. For a PDF, click Print / PDF and choose Save as PDF as the printer.

Profit: gross profit = sales after discounts and returns − the cost of the goods sold (the cost of the FIFO batches used). Net operating profit = gross profit − expenses.

Dashboard

The dashboard shows today's figures: sales, purchases, expenses, gross and net profit, stock value, low-stock products and best sellers. Staff without report rights see only counts, not money figures.

Filter row at the top: choose the Branch (if you work in more than one) and the From and To dates, then click Show. The chosen branch and period are shown at the right. Administrators can choose All branches; this adds a Branches compared chart with each branch's sales, profit and stock value.

Cards use the menu colours: green for selling, orange for buying, purple for money, blue for stock and amber for low stock.

  • Sales per day: one bar per day. With only one day chosen, it shows the last 14 days. For more than two months, it shows one bar per month. Point at a bar to see the amount.
  • Where the money went: net sales, the cost of the goods sold, expenses and the profit that is left.
  • Best-selling products: the longest bar sold the most.
  • How customers paid and Stock value by category: the share of each part. Small parts are grouped as Other.
  • Low stock: how full each product is compared with its minimum. Red means none left.

Administration (administrators only)

Open it with the ⚙ Admin button at the top of every page, or with Users, settings & backup at the bottom of the side menu. The tabs at the top of the page switch between the sections below.

  • Users: create one login per person and tick the branches they work in (an administrator works in all). To stop someone logging in, untick Active. To set a new password, type it and save. If someone forgot their password, open the user and click Reset to default password: the password becomes 123456, and the person must choose a new one when they log in. Usernames must be unique across all businesses.
  • Role permissions: tick what Sales Attendants and Inventory Managers may do, for example give discounts, sell on credit (unpaid or partly paid sales), cancel sales or see profit.
  • Business profile: name, address, phone, email, TIN, logo (PNG or JPG, up to 1 MB), currency, payment methods, units of measure and receipt footer.
  • Backup: each business is backed up separately. A copy of the data is made automatically every day. Click Make and download a backup now and keep copies on a USB stick.
  • Restore: choose a backup file and click Restore backup. Everyone then logs in again.
  • Branches: add a branch with a short code (used in document numbers) and tick on each user's page which branches they work in. A closed branch keeps its history.
  • Businesses (Super Admin only): add another business with its name, a short code and its first administrator's login, or close and reopen one. Each business has its own data, profile, logo, branches and users, and cannot see the others. A closed business keeps its data, but nobody can log in to it. The administrators of the first business are the Super Admins.
  • Audit log: shows who did what, such as logins, price changes, cancellations, returns and adjustments.

Questions and problems

Other computers or phones can't open the app.
Use the address shown in the black start window, for example http://192.168.1.10:5000, on the same Wi-Fi. Allow Python through the Windows firewall for private networks. The start window must stay open.
"Not enough stock" when selling.
The purchase is still a draft. Confirm it to put the goods in stock, or correct the stock with Adjust stock.
"This form was already submitted".
The page was sent twice, for example by a double click or a refresh. The first submission was saved, so nothing is missing.
"Form expired".
Reload the page and try again.
I see "Not allowed".
Your role doesn't have that permission. Ask the administrator.
I can't see the sales or stock of another branch.
Pages show the branch in the top bar. Pick another branch there, if you work in it. Administrators can use All branches on the dashboard and in reports.
A transfer is still "In transit".
The receiving branch must open it and click Receive. Switch to that branch in the top bar first.
The app was started twice.
Run start.bat only once. If the app is already open, just use the browser. To restart, close the black window first.
Forgot a password.
Ask an administrator to open you in ⚙ Admin → Users and click Reset to default password. Log in with 123456, then choose a new password.
